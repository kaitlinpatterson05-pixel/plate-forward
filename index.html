How the lights work

  1. Every night at midnight (Melbourne time) the wall resets and all 100 lights switch off.
  2. Each shift booked that day turns on the next light, and it stays on for the rest of the day.
  3. The more people who book, the brighter the wall gets before it starts fresh the next day.

Our light mission

My final-year university industrial design project was posed to design for the people. I resolved my research to end up designing an interactive light fixture that raises awareness of food insecurity and food waste in Melbourne.

I have always loved food and think its a pivotal way to bring people together, so it is difficult to accept that something so essential to everyday life is not easily accessible to everyone.

I wanted to create a design that makes this issue more visible, encourages people to get involved and demonstrates how small individual actions can contribute to meaningful change. I believe everyone deserves access to nutritious food, and design has the potential to bring communities together and help make this a reality.

0 of 100 lights on today

The wall resets every night at midnight, so each day starts dark and lights up with that day's bookings.

Plastic waste is closely connected to food waste, as surplus food often comes with plastic packaging that contributes to environmental pollution. Australia produces almost 3 million tonnes of plastic annually, while globally, only 15% of plastic is collected for recycling and just 9% is successfully recycled. In my project, I have incorporated recycled plastic to give discarded materials a second life, reflecting the project's broader aim of reducing waste and promoting more sustainable use of existing resources.

More we love

Secondbite

SecondBite is an Australian food-rescue organisation that works to reduce food waste and food insecurity by collecting surplus, edible food from retailers, manufacturers, farmers and other food businesses and redistributing it to people experiencing hardship through community food-relief organisations.

https://secondbite.org

Wonki

Wonki is an Australian sustainable beverage brand founded in 2022 by three Monash University students. The brand tackles food waste by rescuing misshapen, marked and surplus Australian fruit that may otherwise be discarded and transforming it into seasonal alcoholic drinks, including vodka sodas.

https://www.wonki.com.au/

Too Good To Go

Too Good To Go is a food-waste reduction brand that connects consumers with businesses selling surplus food at discounted prices through a mobile app. Its Surprise Bags make food rescue affordable and accessible while helping businesses reduce waste. Through its simple platform and approachable branding, the brand encourages more sustainable consumer choices.

https://www.toogoodtogo.com
